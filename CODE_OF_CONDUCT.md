# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome different viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept feedback with empathy.
- Focus discussions on improving the project.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and other disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner using the contact information on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/NicoGrassetto). Do not include sensitive details in a public issue.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior does not meet these standards.
